Our Dual Concept

Books & Botanical Living Under One Roof

Located along historic Market Street in Leesburg, Virginia (Suite 1 at 11 W Market St & 9 W Market St), Birch Tree Bookstore & Foliage Plant Shop brings together two of life’s greatest simple pleasures: captivating books and thriving greenery.

Whether you are browsing our curated fiction and local interest shelves at Suite 1 or picking up tropical foliage and ceramic pots at 9 W Market St, our store offers an inspiring space to slow down, connect, and discover.
